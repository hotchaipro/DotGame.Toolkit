# Sample content

Where each file in this folder comes from. Everything is made for DotGame and is under the
repository's [MIT license](../../LICENSE), except where a row says otherwise.

| File | Source |
|---|---|
| `Sprites/character_*.png` | The character's idle and jump frames, made by `make-content.py` from `Source/character.png` |
| `Source/character.png` | DotGame.Framework's sample character, by Hot Chai Games LLC, under the MIT license |
| `Sprites/*.png` (the coins, gems, heart, star and panel) | Procedural, made by `make-content.py` |
| `Sounds/*.wav` (blip, chime, coin, jump and pop) | Synthesized by `make-content.py` |
| `LaunchScreen/logo.png`, and the app icons in `../desktop`, `../ios`, `../android` and `../webgl` | The character on a procedural gradient, made by `make-content.py` from `Source/character.png` |
| `Fonts/Inter-Medium.ttf` | Inter by Rasmus Andersson, Copyright 2016 The Inter Project Authors (https://github.com/rsms/inter), under the SIL Open Font License 1.1: see `Fonts/OFL.txt` |
